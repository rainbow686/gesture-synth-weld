🔍 What is a gesture synth?

A gesture synth — a hand gesture synth in full — replaces the keyboard with movement. Instead of pressing keys, you move your hands in the air: a sensor — a webcam, a depth camera, or a sensor glove — reads the position of your hands and fingers, and software maps that motion onto musical parameters like pitch, volume and tone. Raise a hand, the volume rises. Spread your fingers, the chord opens up.

Gesture Synth Weld is a browser-based gesture synth with a fixed two-hand division: your left hand picks the harmony (finger count selects scale degrees I–VII, wrist tilt picks major or minor) while your right hand shapes the expression (height is volume, tilt is tone, finger count sets chord thickness). Nothing to install, nothing to buy — if your device has a camera, you already own the instrument. The full gesture tutorial maps every move.

❓ Gesture synth FAQ

What is a gesture synth?

A musical instrument played with hand or body gestures instead of keys: a sensor reads your movements and software turns them into sound. Gesture Synth Weld is a free browser-based one — your webcam tracks your hands and each finger maps to a different note.

Do I need hardware to play?

Not here. Hardware gesture instruments use sensor gloves or depth cameras — but this site needs only a webcam and a modern browser. No installs, no accounts, nothing to buy. Prefer keys? Desktop keyboard mode plays the same synth with physical keys.

Is Gesture Synth Weld the same as a “gesture synth”?

Gesture Synth Weld is one specific gesture synth: a two-hand browser instrument — left hand harmony, right hand expression — with recording built in. “Gesture synth” is the generic category, and it includes hardware gloves, DIY kits and DAW plugins too.

Can I record what I play?

Yes — up to 120 seconds per take: audio only, full video with the hand-tracking overlay, or a privacy-friendly skeleton animation. Download the file or share it straight to apps on mobile.

Is there a gesture synth app or online version?

This page is the online version — no app to install. Open the instrument in any modern browser on laptop or phone, allow the camera, and you’re playing. That’s the whole point of a browser-based hand gesture synth: the URL is the app.

Can I build my own?

Yes — this site is MIT-licensed open source on GitHub. The stack is MediaPipe hand tracking plus a Tone.js synth, all running locally in the browser.
