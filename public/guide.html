How to Play Gesture Synth Weld

Gesture Synth Weld is a free, browser-based hand gesture music synthesizer — this page is the official tutorial. Your left hand picks the chord, your right hand shapes the sound. No downloads, no hardware, no accounts: open gesturesynthweld.com, allow the camera, and play.

▶ Play Gesture Synth Weld now

⚡ Quick start (30 seconds)

  1. Allow camera — click "Enable Camera" and grant permission. Your video stays on your device; nothing is uploaded.
  2. Left hand: show fingers — 1–5 fingers play scale degrees I–V. See the full table below.
  3. Right hand: join in — both hands must be visible for sound. Raise your right hand for volume.

Defaults out of the box: key of A, Major locked, fixed Major-Triad chord style. Change them in Settings or the toolbar.

👈 Left hand — harmony (scale degree)

Each finger count is a different scale degree (example in A Major):

🤚 Major / minor via wrist tilt

By default the mode is locked to Major ("Scale notes only"). Switch to Scale+Tilt in Settings and your left wrist becomes the switch: tilt left = minor, tilt right = major. The toolbar key selector (C–B) transposes everything.

👉 Right hand — expression

In Finger Layout mode, finger count sets chord thickness:

FingersMajorMinor
1Major TriadMinor Triad
2Major 1st InversionMinor 1st Inversion
3Major 7thMinor 7th
4Major 9thMinor 9th

The default is Fixed Chord Style (Major Triad) — the right hand then controls only volume and tone, and you can lock any of 8 styles (Major Triad, Major 1st Inv, Minor Triad, Diminished, Sus2, Sus4, Major 7th, Dominant 7th) in Settings.

✋ Volume, tone, octave

  • Hand height = volume — higher is louder.
  • Wrist tilt = tone — tilt right for brighter (filter opens), left for darker.
  • Thumb out = octave down — drop a full octave for bass lines; tuck it back for normal.

⏹ Stopping the sound

  • Left fist mutes (notes held, resumes when fingers return).
  • Space stops all notes; Esc resets.
  • A hand leaving the frame stops sound after a short grace period.

🎹 Other modes

  • Theremin — right-hand height = pitch (C3→C6), left-hand height = volume. Continuous gliding, no chords.
  • Mono Piano — left-hand finger count picks the note (root / 3rd / 5th / octave / 9th), right hand triggers it.
  • Keyboard mode (no camera, desktop) — enable in Settings or the toolbar ⌨ button. Keys 1–7 play degrees I–VII (hold to play, release to stop), [ / ] minor/major, 8/9/0/- chord style, Shift octave down, ↑/↓ volume, ←/→ filter, Space stop. Recording is audio-only.

⚙ Settings worth knowing

  • Arpeggiator (Off/Slow/Normal/Fast) — sweeps chord notes like a harp.
  • Auto Bass — root note two octaves down with its own volume.
  • Metronome — BPM 40–240, several time signatures and click sounds (audible in recordings when on).

🔴 Recording your performance

Press Record: a 3-2-1 countdown, up to 120 seconds, a 3-2-1 wrap-up before it stops. Capture audio only, full video (camera + neon skeleton), or skeleton animation (privacy-friendly, no camera feed). Aspect ratios 9:16 / 16:9 / 1:1 for TikTok, YouTube, Instagram. Enable the mic to sing over your chords, then download — or share straight to apps on mobile. Finished takes also auto-save to My works in your browser.

❓ Gesture Synth Weld FAQ

  • Is Gesture Synth Weld free? Yes — free to play and record, no sign-up. MIT-licensed open source (GitHub).
  • Do I need a camera? No — Keyboard mode (desktop) plays with physical keys only.
  • Which browsers? Chrome 90+, Edge 90+, Firefox 90+, Safari 15+. Chrome/Edge recommended for the smoothest tracking.
  • Is my video uploaded? Never — all tracking runs locally on your device.
  • What is Gesture Synth Weld vs a generic "gesture synth"? Gesture Synth Weld is this site's two-hand instrument (left = harmony, right = expression) with 3 modes, arpeggiator, auto bass and built-in recording — not a hardware glove or a DAW plugin.
